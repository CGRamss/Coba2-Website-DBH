DETROIT // 2038

WHEN MACHINES
START TO FEEL.

Detroit has entered a new technological age. Androids are everywhere: working, serving, caring and living alongside humans. But when some of them begin to behave as though they are alive, the boundary between machine and person starts to collapse.

You experience this world through three very different androids. Connor investigates the growing deviant cases. Kara searches for safety and freedom while protecting Alice. Markus becomes the face of a movement demanding a future for androids.
